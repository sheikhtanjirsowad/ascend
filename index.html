Mental health first

Safety & Mental Health

Appearance obsession can be a real mental-health issue. "Looksmaxxing" communities can push extreme, repetitive self-scrutiny. If you:

  • Spend hours analyzing your face or comparing yourself
  • Feel that a specific flaw is ruining your life or how you see yourself
  • Consider harmful or DIY procedures

...you may be experiencing body dysmorphic disorder (BDD) symptoms. This is a recognized, treatable condition — not a character flaw.

Please talk to a mental-health professional or your doctor. If you're in crisis, reach out to a local helpline. Some resources:

  • Your country's distress/suicide hotline — search "mental health helpline" + your city/country.
  • International: Find a Helpline — findahelpline.com
  • APA psychologist locator — locator.apa.org
A healthy relationship with this topic: The evidence-backed fundamentals improve health and confidence. They are not a path to obsession, self-harm, or a guarantee of any outcome. If pursuing "the plan" ever stops feeling healthy, step back.
